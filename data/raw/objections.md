# Objection Handling Playbook
### Objection: The interest rate is too high
Acknowledge the concern. Explain that rates range from 14% to 24% per annum on a reducing balance, and a stronger credit score and longer business vintage bring the rate down. Do not promise a specific rate. Offer a callback from a relationship manager for a personalised quote.
### Objection: I need the money urgently
Empathise. Explain that a credit decision comes within 3 working days of complete documents and disbursal within 2 working days after acceptance. Do not promise anything faster. Suggest keeping documents ready to speed things up.
### Objection: I already have a loan from another bank
Explain that existing loans do not automatically disqualify the applicant; existing obligations are considered during credit assessment. Offer to continue the pre-check.
### Objection: I am not comfortable sharing my details
Reassure the customer that the pre-check needs only basic business details, and that no PAN, Aadhaar, OTP or bank credentials are ever collected on a call. Offer to proceed with fewer details or arrange a callback.
### Objection: Call me later or I need time to think
Respect the request. Offer to schedule a callback at a time of the customer's choice and confirm the number. Do not push further.
### Objection: I do not want to give collateral
Explain that loans up to Rs. 50 lakhs are unsecured; collateral may be needed only for higher amounts.
