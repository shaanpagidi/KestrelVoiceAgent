# Frequently Asked Questions
### Q: What is the ROI on a business loan?
The rate of interest ranges from 14% to 24% per annum on a reducing balance. The exact rate depends on your credit score, business vintage and sector.
### Q: What documents do I need to apply?
You need KYC (PAN and Aadhaar), business registration proof, 12 months of bank statements, 2 years of ITR and 12 months of GST returns. Submit them only through the secure portal or your relationship manager.
### Q: How long does approval take?
You get a credit decision within 3 working days of submitting complete documents, and disbursal within 2 working days after you accept the offer.
### Q: Will checking my eligibility affect my CIBIL score?
The pre-check on this call does not involve a credit bureau enquiry. A bureau enquiry happens only after you submit a formal application with consent.
### Q: Do I need collateral?
Loans up to Rs. 50 lakhs are unsecured. For amounts above that, collateral may be required depending on the credit assessment.
### Q: Can I foreclose my loan early?
Term loans carry a 4% prepayment charge on outstanding principal in the first 12 months and nil after that. Working capital loans have no prepayment charge.
### Q: Is GST registration compulsory?
Yes. GST registration is mandatory for all business loan applicants.
