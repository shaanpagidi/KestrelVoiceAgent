# Business Loan Credit Policy
Confidential - internal use only
## Eligibility Criteria
### Applicant and business
- Eligible entities are proprietorships, partnership firms, LLPs and private limited companies.
- Minimum business vintage is 2 years.
- Minimum annual turnover of Rs. 25 lakhs as per the latest ITR or GST returns.
- GST registration is mandatory.
- Applicant age must be between 25 and 65 years at application.
### Credit score
A credit score of 700 or above qualifies for standard processing. A credit score between 650 and 699 is referred for manual review. Applications below 650 cannot be processed.
### Loan amount
Loan amount ranges from Rs. 5 lakhs to Rs. 1 crore. For working capital loans the indicative maximum is 50% of annual turnover.
## Pricing and Fees
The interest rate is 14% to 24% per annum on a reducing balance, based on credit score, business vintage and sector. The processing fee is 1% to 2% of the loan amount plus GST.
Prepayment on term loans: 4% of outstanding principal if prepaid within the first 12 months, nil thereafter. Working capital loans have nil prepayment charges.
Late payment charge is 2% per month on the overdue amount.
## Documents Required
KYC of the applicant (PAN and Aadhaar), business registration proof, last 12 months bank statements, last 2 years ITR and last 12 months GST returns.
Customers must submit documents only through the secure portal or their relationship manager, never over a phone call.
## Process and Timelines
Steps: application, document verification, credit assessment, offer, disbursal. A credit decision is given within 3 working days of receiving complete documents. Disbursal happens within 2 working days of offer acceptance.
## Compliance Rules for Calling
Agents must never guarantee approval and must state that eligibility is preliminary. Agents must not collect OTP, PAN, Aadhaar or bank credentials on calls. Calls are recorded and the customer must be informed. Do-not-call requests must be honoured immediately.
